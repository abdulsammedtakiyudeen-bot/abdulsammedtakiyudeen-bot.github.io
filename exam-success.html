💡 Study Tips for Exam Success

  1. Create a realistic revision timetable.
  2. Study topics in small, manageable sections.
  3. Practise past questions regularly.
  4. For theory questions, answer the exact command word.
  5. Show your working in mathematical calculations.
  6. Check your answers and manage your examination time.
  7. Rest properly and ask teachers about difficult topics.
Remember: No video or study method can guarantee a pass. Consistent practice, understanding, and preparation can improve your chances of success.
